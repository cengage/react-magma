## Designing with Magma

Magma gives designers a shared set of foundations, components, and guidance for creating consistent Cengage experiences efficiently.

Cengage’s visual language is expressed through color, typography, spacing, layout, iconography, and other foundational decisions. Magma translates that language into reusable design assets and coded components that teams can apply across products.

Start by becoming familiar with the foundations of the system, then use the component guidelines to understand how individual interface elements should look, behave, and work together.

---

## Explore the guidelines

Use the design documentation to understand the breadth of Magma and make informed decisions when creating Cengage experiences.

### Foundations

Learn about the core visual and structural elements of Magma, including [color](/design-intro/colors/), [typography](/design-intro/typography/), [spacing](/design-intro/spacing/), [layout](/design-intro/layout/), and [icons](/design/icon/). These foundations help products maintain a consistent expression of the Cengage brand.

### Components

Components are reusable interface elements designed to create consistent experiences across Cengage products. Each component’s guidelines explain when to use it, how it should behave, and the accessibility considerations to keep in mind.

### Data visualization

Use Magma’s [data-visualization](/data-visualization/introduction/) guidance to communicate information clearly and accurately. The guidance covers accessible chart foundations, recommended chart types, and examples for presenting data effectively.

At Cengage we use Figma for our primary design tool and we have component libraries available for you to use. To access these libraries you'll need to sign in to the Cengage Figma organization using your Cengage email address.

If you have any questions or issues, please reach out in the #react-magma Slack channel.

## Guiding Principles

Cengage helps students and instructors realize their potential and achieve their goals. Magma supports that mission by helping teams build relevant, intuitive tools and effective learning experiences centered on the needs of our users. The following design principles guide the decisions we make.

  <p>
    Don't overburden me with options or choice. Proactively assume what I need
    and when, while equipping me with the ability to change it.
  </p>
  <BlockQuote>
    <BlockQuoteItem>I hate going through each student or each activity and changing things when I'm changing the same thing ... on 10, 20 or 30 of them. It is so time consuming.</BlockQuoteItem>

  </BlockQuote>
</div>
<div className="principles-c">
  <h3>Tolerate Mistakes</h3>
  <p>
    Mistakes are proof that I’m trying, but they should never impede my
    education experience. Preventing and helping me recover from a mistake
    should be as easy as it was to make it.
  </p>
  <BlockQuote>
    <BlockQuoteItem>...I feel like I am not learning with this software because I have no way of knowing where I made my mistakes.</BlockQuoteItem>
    <BlockQuoteItem hasAttribution>SAM Student</BlockQuoteItem>
  </BlockQuote>
</div>
<div className="principles-c">
  <h3>Inspire Confidence</h3>
  <p>
    Build confidence at every opportunity. Confidence that I can improve my
    understanding, confidence that the paragraph I just wrote won’t get lost and
    confidence that I won’t get lost.
  </p>
  <BlockQuote>
    <BlockQuoteItem>I’m calling in because all of my material on my account is gone.</BlockQuoteItem>
    <BlockQuoteItem hasAttribution>MindTap Instructor</BlockQuoteItem>
  </BlockQuote>
</div>
<div className="principles-c">
  <h3>Cultivate Focus</h3>
  <p>
    Distractions are plentiful and my undivided attention is scarce. Any

    experience.
  </p>

    <BlockQuoteItem>... You also put up an online pop-up advertisement that would not go away for a solid week and prevented me from being able to complete the homework for that week at a reasonable pace.</BlockQuoteItem>

</div>
<div className="principles-c">
  <h3>Preserve Simplicity</h3>
  <p>
    Be intentionally simple. Making common tasks fast, and easy for me to
    understand without explanation allows me to concentrate on my education
    experience.
  </p>
  <BlockQuote>
    <BlockQuoteItem>The organization was complicated and it was difficult to find assignments that were due...</BlockQuoteItem>

  </BlockQuote>

<div className="principles-c">
  <h3>Encourage Mobility</h3>
  <p>
    I am not constrained by device or location and I expect the same of my
    education experience. Mobile empowers me to succeed, regardless of location,
    device or time.
  </p>
